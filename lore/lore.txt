The realm of Tenebrous is a land shrouded in eternal twilight.
The ancient castle of Kragnir lies to the north, cursed by dark magic.
Goblins in Tenebrous are cunning creatures who worship the Shadow God.
The forest of Elvendom is home to corrupted treants and shadow wolves.
The artifact known as the Void Crystal can amplify dark magic tenfold.
Warriors in Tenebrous are known for their blood oath tradition.
Mages draw power from the shadow ley lines running beneath the earth.
Archers of the realm use arrows tipped with moonstone to pierce dark creatures.
Rogues belong to the secret guild called the Phantom Hand.
